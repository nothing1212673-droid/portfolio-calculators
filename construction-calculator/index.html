Each selected work package covers the full project area. Base renovation includes preparation, carpentry, general installation and site management. Flooring, painting, electrical and plumbing are separate optional packages, excluded from the base rate.

Sample sales tax = 8% of materials. Reserve = 10% of materials + labor + tax, rounded to cents. Total = materials + labor + tax + reserve.

1 sq ft = 0.09290304 m². Switching units preserves the physical area and the estimate. Valid area: 1–100,000 sq ft (0.09290304–9,290.304 m²). Permits, design fees, demolition, structural work and local code requirements are excluded. Actual rates and taxes vary by location.
